Interactive anatomy lab

Allows users to explore 3D models of the human body on a computer, tablet or VR headset. Users could rotate organs, zoom in, hide anatomical layers and select structures to reveal labels and explanations. For example, an interactive heart model could let students explore its chambers, valves and blood vessels, watch an animation of blood flow and test their knowledge through identification activities

Surgical training and Stimulation

Creating a virtual environment where learners can practise procedures and develop an understanding of surgical anatomy. Users could interact with 3D organs, manipulate virtual instruments and follow guided procedural steps. For example, a cardiac simulation could allow learners to practise navigating a catheter through a simplified model of blood vessels and the heart.

AI/Machine Learning Histology classification

An application that analyses digital images of tissue samples and identifies patterns, such as different tissue types or potentially abnormal regions. Users could upload a histology image and view predicted classifications, highlighted areas and confidence scores. This could support education and research, while clinical use would require rigorous validation and professional oversight.
