About me

🇦🇺 Junior Full-stack developer, based in Australia

As a Junior Developer in healthcare technology, I focus on building interfaces that are clear, accessible, and reliable for clinical users. I work with HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and modern frameworks like React to create responsive dashboards, patient‑facing portals, and data‑driven components that support medical workflows. I pay close attention to accuracy, usability, and compliance, ensuring UI elements handle sensitive information safely and consistently. I also collaborate with backend and data teams to integrate APIs, visualise health metrics, and support AI‑driven features in digital health platforms. This combination of technical skill and user‑centred design helps me contribute to software that clinicians and patients can trust.
In immersive and simulation technology, I apply front‑end skills to build interactive experiences that feel smooth, intuitive, and visually engaging. I work with JavaScript, TypeScript, WebGL, and frameworks like Three.js to create real‑time visual components, 3D interfaces, and simulation‑driven UI elements. I focus on performance, ensuring animations, controls, and data updates run efficiently in environments such as VR/AR training systems or real‑time simulation dashboards. I enjoy collaborating with multidisciplinary teams—designers, simulation engineers, and graphics specialists—to translate complex systems into clear, interactive front‑end experiences. This allows me to support immersive products that are both technically strong and easy for users to navigate.
